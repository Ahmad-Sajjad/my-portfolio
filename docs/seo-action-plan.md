## How AI/SEO actually evaluates "is this the right Ahmad Sajjad?"

When Perplexity/ChatGPT picks a result, it looks for converging signals:

1. **Domain authority** — How trusted is each citing source?
   - High: LinkedIn, GitHub, Crunchbase, Wikipedia, .edu, .gov, major news
   - Medium: Reivex.io, Product Hunt, Hashnode, dev.to, Medium
   - Low: Your own portfolio, personal blogs

2. **Mutual citation** — Do the high-authority sources link to each other and to you?
   - LinkedIn → Reivex.io → ahmadsajjad.dev → LinkedIn = closed loop = trusted

3. **Consistent claims** — Do they agree on the facts?
   - All sources agreeing "Ahmad Sajjad, co-founder Reivex, Lahore, UET" → strong
   - One source says "founder Ahad", another says "co-founders Ahmad & Ahad" → AI defaults to the higher-authority claim (currently Reivex.io itself)

4. **Disambiguation signals** — Specific identifiers other Ahmads don't share
   - UET Lahore ✓
   - CodeRush 2026 winner ✓
   - Founded Reivex Technologies ✓ (once that's in 3+ places)
   - Project names: PowerSell, India Rare ✓
